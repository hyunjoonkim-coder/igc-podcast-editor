Site-wide brand assets
======================

Put files in this folder and list their names in brand.json. Everyone who opens the
site will then use them automatically. (Files someone adds on the "Brand assets"
page in their own browser take priority over these.)

Example brand.json:
{
  "bumper": "InGoodCompany_Intro.mp4",
  "music": "intro_music.m4a",
  "outro": "InGoodCompany_EPOutro.mp4",
  "title_background": "title_bg.png",
  "logo": "logo.png",
  "font_title": "Title-Bold.otf",
  "font_bold": "Sans-Bold.otf",
  "font_regular": "Sans-Regular.otf"
}

Videos should be H.264 MP4 (or H.264 MOV). Music can be WAV, MP3 or M4A.
